Our story

Built in Nanded, for Nanded.

For a long time, a family in and around Nerli who wanted serious coaching had to choose: travel for swimming, travel again for cricket, and give up on shooting entirely.

Skill For Glory was created to end that. We put a proper swimming pool, a supervised shooting range and two floodlit turfs on one campus, staffed them with coaches who teach rather than merely supervise, and opened seven days a week so training fits around school and work instead of fighting them.

The emblem says it plainly — Sports Education, Essential Skills for Life. Some of our athletes will go on to district and state competition. Most will not, and that is entirely fine. Every one of them leaves with discipline, fitness, and the habit of working at something difficult until it becomes easy.

What we stand for

Four commitments we hold ourselves to

These are not posters on a wall. They are the standards a parent can hold us to on any given day.

Safety first, always

A lifeguard on deck, a range officer on the line and first aid on site. No session runs unsupervised, ever.

Measured progress

A written skills ladder per sport, periodic assessment and a progress card you can actually read.

Open to everyone

Separate ladies' batches, beginner-only timings and concessions for siblings. Sport should not be a closed door.

Timings we keep

Published schedules, batches that start on time and slots that are not double-booked. Reliability is a service.

Coaching team

Coaches who correct, not just count laps

Every coach at SFG is certified in their discipline, trained in first aid and background-verified before they stand in front of a batch.

Head Coach — Swimming

Learn-to-swim & squad

Runs the beginner ladder and the squad sets, and holds the lifeguard qualification for the pool.

Range Officer — Shooting

Rifle & pistol

Controls the firing line, delivers the safety briefing and coaches the technical fundamentals.

Head Coach — Cricket

Batting, bowling & fielding

Plans the nets programme, individual technical work and the inter-batch match calendar.

Head Coach — Football

Technique & small-sided games

Builds the ball-mastery syllabus and the weekly small-sided game programme on the turf.

Want to know who will be coaching your child? Ask at reception — we are happy to introduce you before you enrol.

Safety & hygiene

The part we refuse to cut corners on.

Parents leave their children with us. That is a serious thing, and we treat it as one.

  • A trained lifeguard is on the pool deck for every session, including free swim and private bookings.
  • Water is filtered and tested on a fixed daily schedule, with a weekly rest-and-clean slot for the pool.
  • The shooting range runs a strict protocol — controlled entry, a briefing before every relay and a range officer on the line throughout.
  • First-aid kit and trained staff on site whenever the academy is open, with the nearest hospital contact posted at reception.
  • Coaches are background-verified and never left alone with a single child.
  • Changing rooms are cleaned daily and monitored, with lockers for valuables during sessions.
