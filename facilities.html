Swimming Pool

A pool that teaches beginners and still challenges a squad swimmer.

The heart of the academy. Water is filtered and tested on a fixed schedule, depth is clearly marked, and a trained lifeguard is on deck for every single session — including free swim.

  • Graded depth with a separate shallow section, so a five-year-old and a squad swimmer can train at the same time in safety.
  • Learn-to-swim ladder — water confidence, floating, kicking, breathing, then freestyle, backstroke, breaststroke and butterfly in sequence.
  • Separate ladies' batches and dedicated beginner timings, published in advance so you always know who is in the water.
  • Filtration and chlorination checked daily, with the pool rested and cleaned on a fixed weekly slot.
  • Changing rooms and showers for men and women, plus a shaded spectator area for waiting parents.

Shooting Range

Precision sport, taught with the discipline it deserves.

Shooting rewards stillness, breath control and patience — the exact qualities that transfer straight into a student's schoolwork. Our range runs on a strict, non-negotiable safety protocol.

  • Fixed firing points with a clear firing line, backstop and controlled entry — nobody moves downrange while the line is hot.
  • A range officer supervises every relay. Safety briefing at the start of each session, every session, no exceptions.
  • Coached fundamentals — stance, grip, sight alignment, trigger release and follow-through, built up slowly and in order.
  • Equipment and targets provided for beginners, so a new student can try the sport before investing in anything.
  • Score tracking across sessions, so improvement is visible on paper rather than guessed at.

Cricket Turf

True bounce, honest nets, and lights that stay on.

Whether you are grooving a defensive push or running a Sunday-morning team practice, the strip plays consistently and the nets are maintained rather than merely present.

  • Practice nets for batting and bowling drills, with space for throwdowns and shadow work alongside.
  • Floodlights for early-morning and post-work sessions — useful nine months of the year in Nanded.
  • Coached batches covering batting technique, seam and spin bowling, fielding drills and match awareness.
  • Private bookings by the hour for teams, corporate groups and box-cricket matches, on a first-confirmed-first-served basis.
  • Seating and shade beside the ground so families and reserves are not standing in the sun.

Football Turf

All-weather synthetic grass that plays the same in June and in August.

A cushioned artificial pitch that drains quickly after rain and does not turn to dust in the dry months — so training runs to schedule instead of to the weather.

  • Quick-draining synthetic surface, playable within minutes of a shower rather than hours.
  • Five-a-side and seven-a-side formats with goals, bibs and balls kept on site.
  • Coaching batches in first touch, passing patterns, finishing, goalkeeping and small-sided game intelligence.
  • Evening league and friendly slots for college teams, office groups and regular five-a-side crews.
  • Perimeter netting and floodlights, so play continues after dark without chasing the ball down the road.
