Three grounds, built to be trained on every day.

A swimming pool, a supervised shooting range and a floodlit all-weather turf for cricket and football — maintained, staffed and open seven days a week at our Nerli campus.

Swimming Pool

A pool that teaches beginners and still challenges a squad swimmer.

The heart of the academy. Water is filtered and tested on a fixed schedule, depth is clearly marked, and a trained lifeguard is on deck for every session — free swim included.

  • Graded depth with a separate shallow section, so a five-year-old and a squad swimmer train safely at the same time.
  • A written learn-to-swim ladder — water confidence, floating, kicking and breathing, then freestyle, backstroke, breaststroke and butterfly in sequence.
  • Separate ladies' batches and dedicated beginner timings, published in advance.
  • Filtration and chlorination checked daily, with a fixed weekly rest-and-clean slot.
  • Changing rooms and showers for men and women, plus shaded seating for waiting parents.

Shooting Range

Precision sport, taught with the discipline it deserves.

Shooting rewards stillness, breath control and patience — the qualities that transfer straight into schoolwork. Our range runs a strict, non-negotiable safety protocol.

  • Fixed firing points with a clear firing line and backstop — nobody moves downrange while the line is hot.
  • A range officer supervises every relay, with a safety briefing at the start of each session. No exceptions.
  • Coached fundamentals — stance, grip, sight alignment, trigger release and follow-through, built up in order.
  • Equipment and targets provided, so a new student can try the sport before investing in anything.
  • Score tracking across sessions, so improvement is visible on paper rather than guessed at.

Cricket & Football Turf

One turf, both games — true bounce by day, five-a-side under lights.

The same all-weather ground serves both games. Cricket nets and a true-bounce strip run through the morning; the turf switches to five- and seven-a-side football in the evening.

  • Cricket practice nets for batting and bowling drills, with room for throwdowns and shadow work alongside.
  • Five- and seven-a-side football on cushioned synthetic grass, with goals, bibs and match balls kept on site.
  • Quick-draining surface that is playable within minutes of a shower and does not turn to dust in the dry months.
  • Floodlights and perimeter netting, so evening sessions run safely without chasing the ball down the road.
  • Private hourly bookings for teams, offices, box-cricket and weekend five-a-side crews.
  • Seating and shade beside the ground, so families and reserves are not standing in the sun.
