Coaching batches

Four sports, one coaching philosophy

Every batch works from a written skills ladder. Athletes move up when the coach signs them off — not automatically at the end of a month.

Swimming

Swimming Coaching

Ages 5+

Learn-to-swim through to stroke correction and squad training. Beginners start in the shallow section with a coach in the water alongside them.

  • Water confidence & floating
  • Freestyle, backstroke, breaststroke, butterfly
  • Stroke correction & timed sets
  • Separate ladies' batches
Enquire about swimming
Shooting

Shooting Coaching

Ages 10+

Rifle and pistol fundamentals taught under a range officer, with score tracking so progress is measured rather than assumed.

  • Safety protocol & range discipline
  • Stance, grip and sight alignment
  • Breath control & trigger release
  • Session-by-session score cards
Enquire about shooting
Cricket

Cricket Coaching

Ages 7+

Net-based technical work plus match play, covering batting, seam and spin bowling, fielding and game awareness.

  • Batting technique & shot selection
  • Seam and spin bowling actions
  • Fielding, catching & throwing drills
  • Practice matches under lights
Enquire about cricket
Football

Football Coaching

Ages 6+

Small-sided coaching on the artificial turf, built around first touch, passing and decision-making rather than laps and drills alone.

  • First touch & ball mastery
  • Passing patterns and movement
  • Finishing & goalkeeping basics
  • Weekly small-sided matches
Enquire about football

Turf booking

Book the cricket or football turf by the hour.

You do not have to be an academy member to play. Teams, offices and weekend groups can block a slot on either turf — floodlights, goals, bibs and balls included.

  • Hourly slots from early morning to late evening, seven days a week.
  • Floodlights included at no extra charge on evening bookings.
  • Equipment on site — goals, bibs, cones and match balls.
  • Confirmed on payment of an advance, so your slot is genuinely held.
  • Regular weekly slots available for teams that play at a fixed time.

What's included

Every membership comes with these as standard

A free trial session

Try the batch in full before you pay anything. Bring the kit you have; we will lend the rest.

A written progress record

Each athlete gets a skills card showing what they have cleared and what comes next.

Supervised, insured sessions

Qualified coaches, a lifeguard on pool deck and first aid on site whenever we are open.

Inter-batch competitions

Regular in-house meets and matches, so athletes compete long before they reach a district trial.

Access to open slots

Members can use free-play and open turf slots at member rates when the ground is free.

Flexible payment

Monthly or quarterly, in cash or by UPI. Quarterly plans carry a discount.
