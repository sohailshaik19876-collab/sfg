SFG Affiliation Program

Turn your school into a Sports Excellence Centre.

A five to ten year partnership in which SFG develops the infrastructure, deploys the coaches and integrates a sports curriculum — without disturbing your academics.

THE PROBLEM

Why schools need this

  • Sport is treated as extra-curricular, so it is never given real importance.
  • Most schools have no valid sports ground or infrastructure.
  • A single PE instructor is expected to handle 300–1000 students.
  • No talent identification or development mechanism exists.
  • Schools struggle to balance academics and sport at the same time.

No infrastructure + no training + no participation = no results.

THE ANSWER

What affiliation gives you

  • International-standard grounds, synthetic courts and approved turfs at nominal cost.
  • High-end training from sport-specific experts, trainers and coaches.
  • In-depth performance analysis for every child.
  • City-wide inter-school leagues and regular events at your campus.
  • 100% participation in district, state and national tournaments.
  • Sports personalities invited to speak with your students.

Outsourced school sports

Hand us your PE programme, and we will run it properly.

SFG takes over the physical education programme and sports activities at a school, college or academy — with a pool of over 1,000 sport-specific trainers, coaches, physiotherapists, nutrition experts and data analysts behind it.

Certification

Skills our students can actually put on a CV

SFG certification is issued through programmes tied to the Ministry of Skill Development and Entrepreneurship, and is recognised beyond the sports field.

SEAP

Sports Education Awards Program. Participants complete nationally recognised workforce assessments developed by SFG. Those who qualify receive a Certificate of Excellence indicating their level of education readiness.

SKILL Ready!

Workforce certification. Job seekers and working people complete the SKILL Ready! assessments to earn a certificate that evidences their work readiness to current and future employers.

CSSET courses

Council of Sports Skill Education and Training. Non-AICTE and non-UGC sports skill courses, designed by experienced professionals against the standards the global market expects.

How we work

Don't be busy — be productive.

Seven steps from the first conversation to the first training session. Nothing is committed until both sides have seen the ground and signed the same document.

Start with a conversation

  1. Discussion

    We start by understanding your ground, your students and what you want sport to do for them.

  2. Inspection

    Our team visits and surveys the site — land, access, drainage, existing facilities.

  3. Second inspection

    A technical re-check before anything is committed to paper. We would rather find a problem now.

  4. MoU signed

    Scope, timelines, responsibilities and costs, agreed in writing before work begins.

  5. Development

    Construction and infrastructure development to international standards.

  6. Implementation

    Coaches deployed, curriculum integrated, equipment installed, batches formed.

  7. Operations begin

    Training starts, performance is tracked, and students enter district and state competition.
