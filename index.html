Our facilities

Three grounds. Four sports. One campus.

Most academies specialise in one sport. We built a pool, a range and an all-weather turf that plays both cricket and football — so a family can train together and an athlete can cross-train without leaving the ground.

Why families choose SFG

Coaching built on safety, structure and steady progress.

Our motto is not decoration. Every batch follows a written progression, and every parent knows what their child is working on this month.

  • Certified coaches with 15–20 years in physical education, plus retired athletes who have competed at the top.
  • Small batch sizes so each athlete gets real correction, not a place in a queue.
  • Physique-based sport selection — we evaluate each student and match them to the discipline they will thrive in.
  • Medical supervision with a physiotherapist and nutrition expert assigned to players.
  • Separate ladies' and beginners' batches, plus dedicated timings for school groups.

How we work

Don't be busy — be productive.

Seven steps from first conversation to the first training session. Nothing is committed until both sides have seen the ground and signed the same document.

About affiliation

  1. Discussion

    We start by understanding your ground, your students and what you want sport to do for them.

  2. Inspection

    Our team visits and surveys the site — land, access, drainage, existing facilities.

  3. Second inspection

    A technical re-check before anything is committed to paper. We would rather find a problem now.

  4. MoU signed

    Scope, timelines, responsibilities and costs, agreed in writing before work begins.

  5. Development

    Construction and infrastructure development to international standards.

  6. Implementation

    Coaches deployed, curriculum integrated, equipment installed, batches formed.

  7. Operations begin

    Training starts, performance is tracked, and students enter district and state competition.
