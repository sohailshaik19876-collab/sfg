Our Facilities

Four disciplines. One campus. Zero compromises.

Most academies specialise in one sport. We built four properly — so a family can train together, and an athlete can cross-train without ever leaving the ground.

Why families choose SFG

Coaching built on safety, structure and steady progress.

Our motto — Sports Education, Essential Skills for Life — is not decoration. Every batch follows a written progression, and every parent knows exactly what their child is working on this month.

  • Certified, background-checked coaches — never an unsupervised session, and a trained lifeguard on deck whenever the pool is open.
  • Small batch sizes so each athlete gets real correction, not a place in a queue.
  • A written skills ladder for every sport, with periodic assessments and a progress card you can actually read.
  • Separate ladies' and beginners' batches in the pool, plus dedicated timings for school groups.
  • Clean changing rooms, drinking water and secure parking — the details that decide whether a family keeps coming back.

Programmes

A pathway for every stage — from first splash to state trials.

Choose the track that fits today. Move up when the coach says you are ready, not when the calendar says so.

Junior Beginners

Ages 5–10. Water confidence, basic ball skills and coordination games. The stage where sport becomes a habit rather than a chore.

Skill Development

Ages 11–16. Technique breakdown, drill work and conditioning across swimming, shooting, cricket and football.

Competition Squad

Selection-based. Periodised training, timed trials and preparation for district, divisional and state-level meets.

Adults & Fitness

Ages 17+. Early-morning lap swimming, evening turf leagues and weekend recreational slots for working professionals.

Getting started

Joining takes about ten minutes.

No long forms and no pressure. Come and see the ground first — most families decide on the spot.

  1. Call or send an enquiry

    Tell us the sport, the age and roughly when you can train. We will suggest the batch that fits.

  2. Visit for a free trial

    Walk the campus, meet the coach and try one full session before paying anything.

  3. Complete a short registration

    ID proof, a photograph and a basic health declaration. Fees can be paid monthly or per quarter.

  4. Start training — and keep score

    Your coach sets the first goals in week one and reviews them with you every month.
